# Ant Arena — pitch teaser: three storylines

Source of every claim: `PRODUCT.md` (pitch, problem table, "Why Ant Arena wins", ready-made copy).
Every screen shown is the real app, captured from the in-browser demo (no mockups).
Characters, pets, rides, logo, palette (Lime #C6F432 · Orange #FF5A1F · Ink #161512 · Paper #F4F3EE · Yellow #FFD84D)
and fonts (Anton, Figtree, Bangers, Comic Neue) come from the repo.
Not used: the forest-arena hero art, because PRODUCT.md notes it resembles another game's style.

---

## A. "Module 4 of 19" (recommended)
**Device:** life before and after, as a moment in one employee's day. **Form:** object-to-object chain, where each shot ends on the thing that opens the next.

An employee faces a 47-minute compliance module and their eyes glaze over. Their phone buzzes: "Your 2-minute sprint is ready · Day 12 🔥". From there one continuous chain carries the whole platform. The answer tile becomes coins, the coins drop onto the 3D Arena Map, and the map's next stop is a comic page. The comic's speech bubble becomes a Live Quiz PIN on a projector, the podium turns into a poll word cloud, the cloud prints a certificate, and the certificate slides onto the manager's knowledge-gap report. Then the whole app re-skins into a second company's brand.

- **First 2 s:** a grey e-learning player reads "Module 4 of 19 · 47 min remaining". The progress bar crawls 1%, a cursor hovers over "Next" and nothing happens.
- **Beats:** pain → buzz → Daily Sprint (swipe sort, buckets, second chance) → coins → 3D map, characters, pets and rides → Story Mode comic → Live Quiz → Polls → Certificate → Admin gaps → white-label swap → claim "Learning your team actually plays" → end card with the logo, "Try it free — no sign-up" and the URL.
- **Look:** Paper ground, ink type in Anton, a lime accent rationed to the payoff, real UI at 1.6–2× size, Bangers only inside the comic world.
- **Sound:** an upbeat 120 bpm track that enters on the phone buzz, plus UI clicks, coin chimes, a crowd cheer at the podium and a stamp on the certificate.
- **Length:** 60–75 s at 16:9 1920×1080.

## B. "Wren's route"
**Device:** a character does the work. **Form:** a flight through one world.

Wren, the app's own chibi character, sets off across the 3D Arena Map. Each landmark on the route is a feature: Wren rides a ride, a pet joins, Wren steps into a comic panel, onto a quiz-show stage and up to a certificate podium. A camera pull-back reveals the whole route sitting inside a phone, then that phone inside a company's branded app.

- **First 2 s:** Wren mid-stride on the lit 3D path with a coin popping overhead, the camera low and travelling.
- **Beats:** Wren sets off → sprint gate → store (pet and ride) → comic world → Live Quiz stage → poll plaza → certificate hall → pull back to phone → manager view → end card.
- **Look:** the map's own sky and lantern ambience, the character in every shot, UI cards appearing as signposts.
- **Sound:** an adventurous, playful track, footsteps, and a chime at each landmark.
- **Length:** 50–70 s.
- **Risk:** it reads charming but less "B2B". Buyers see less of the manager and security value.

## C. "From compulsory to competitive" (the bold one)
**Device:** type carries the argument. **Form:** cut on the beat.

Giant kinetic words in Anton against hard colour fields that cut every half-beat ("Compulsory." "Boring." "Unmeasured."), then a flip: "Competitive." Each word punches into one real UI moment at full frame, two seconds or less each. Every feature gets one verb.

- **First 2 s:** the word "COMPULSORY" fills the frame in ink on grey, gets stamped, and cracks.
- **Beats:** three pain words → flip → twelve features as verb/UI hits (Sprint · Sort · Retry · Race · Read · Host · Vote · Certify · Measure · Brand · Secure · Launch) → claim → end card.
- **Look:** full-bleed Lime, Orange and Ink colour fields with Anton type at 300+ px and real UI punch-ins.
- **Sound:** a driving 128 bpm track with hard cuts on the beat.
- **Length:** 35–45 s. This makes the best social teaser but has less room to explain.
