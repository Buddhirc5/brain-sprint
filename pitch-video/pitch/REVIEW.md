# Review: pitch A "Module 4 of 19"

**Creative calls**
- The story is before/after. A grey e-learning module (a generic window, not a real product) fails on screen, then the phone rises in and the Paper world floods over it.
- The phone is the carrier through scenes 2–4. After that, each feature gets its own world:
  - Lime for rewards
  - Yellow halftone comic for Story Mode
  - The big screen for the live quiz
  - The admin desk for the dashboard
- Every product screen is a real capture from the demo, rebranded to Ant Arena. Nothing in the product shots is drawn.
- Storylines A and C were built. Storyline B ("Wren's route") was not.

**Measured**
- `check` passes, with one warning kept on purpose: the greyed-out "Next" button in the old-way scene is low contrast because it is meant to look disabled.
- The draft render is 73.0 s. Audio: −17.8 LUFS integrated, −1.7 dBTP true peak, no clipping. The delivery file is loudness-normalised to about −15 LUFS.
- The review sheets (beats, seams, phone width) were read, and seam fixes were applied: no blank frames between scenes.

**Not checked**
- The sound was measured, not heard.
- Kokoro is a free, on-device voice. It is clear but slightly synthetic. A HeyGen voice or a recorded human read would lift the film the most.

**Weakest shot**
- Scene 10 (admin): the camera move across the dashboard is the least "story" beat.
- With more time I would show one manager filtering by team and the gap number changing.

**Warnings kept**
- The silence gaps between voice lines (about 1–1.8 s) are there because there is no music bed. Adding music would fill them.
