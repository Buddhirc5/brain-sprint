# Arena Map sound credits

Music (`music-*.mp3`, one theme per land) was generated with Google Lyria (RealTime music model) for this app.

Nature recordings and animal calls, trimmed, looped and level-matched for the map:

- `call-sheep.mp3`: "Sheep bleating.ogg" by earthcalling, Public domain, from Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sheep_bleating.ogg)
- `amb-meadow.mp3`: "Corner of a sheep field in summer.ogg" by earthcalling, Public domain, from Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Corner_of_a_sheep_field_in_summer.ogg)
- `call-gull.mp3`: "Gull 2.ogg" by avphillips, Public domain, from Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gull_2.ogg)
- `call-splash.mp3`: "Bathtub water splashes.ogg" by gradha, Public domain, from Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bathtub_water_splashes.ogg)
- `amb-waves.mp3`: "Small sea waves at rocky beach.opus" by mnalis, CC BY 3.0, from Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Small_sea_waves_at_rocky_beach.opus)
- `amb-pond.mp3`: "Nature sounds ambience in a Dordogne pond.ogg" by Glaneur de sons, CC BY 3.0, from Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nature_sounds_ambience_in_a_Dordogne_pond.ogg)
- `amb-bubbles.mp3`: "Toberbreedia water bubbling" by A.-K. D., cc0, from Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?curid=152439105)

The pop and chime used for players come from `src/assets/sound` (see its CREDITS.md).
